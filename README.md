# Wrestling Chronology

An open-source media player for wrestling history buffs exploring historical programmes and recorded collections on Internet Archive and other public video sites. Browse available recordings, choose a year and watch in chronological order.

The app focuses on wrestling history and prerecorded events. It does not provide live event broadcasts.

Wrestling Chronology is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the featured wrestling promotions or video platforms.

## Media sources

The catalogue draws on collections and source links contributed by wrestling fans around the world. These include recordings available on Internet Archive, alongside official YouTube channels and public videos on Dailymotion and OK.ru. Wrestling Chronology organises these contributions so available recordings can be browsed and watched in chronological order.

The app creator does not upload these recordings or maintain a separate hosted video library. Recordings are retrieved on demand from external providers, with some streams forwarded through project-operated delivery services.

Collections, playlists and uploader pages are linked in the repository so you can trace each source. Availability depends on the original uploader and hosting platform. Rights in recordings remain with their respective owners; inclusion in the catalogue does not imply ownership, endorsement or permission from a rightsholder.

## Copyright concerns

Requests to remove an original upload can be submitted through the relevant provider's copyright-reporting process:

- **Internet Archive:** [Copyright information and policy](https://archivesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014759692-Rights)
- **YouTube:** [Submit a copyright removal request](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2807622?hl=en)
- **Dailymotion:** [Report a copyright infringement](https://faq.dailymotion.com/hc/en-us/articles/203657336-Report-a-copyright-infringement)
- **OK.ru:** [Copyright complaints and reporting form](https://ok.ru/help/narushenie-avtorskih-prav)

For concerns about a link or playback source included in Wrestling Chronology, email **[wc-complaints@protonmail.com](mailto:wc-complaints@protonmail.com)**. Please include the episode or collection, the source URL, your relationship to the rights owner, and details of your concern.

We will review reports and take appropriate action concerning links or playback access provided by the app. We cannot remove recordings from external platforms. You do not need to contact the hosting provider first or join Discord to report a concern to us.
