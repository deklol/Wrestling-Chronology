A metadata organiser and general-purpose video player, built by wrestling fans.

Play your own local video files and organise a personal library on your device, independently of any online catalogue.

WC Player organises links and metadata collected and shared by fans around the world, including members of our community. These contributions cover public archives, collections and playlists, helping people browse available videos in chronological order and choose what to play from external sources, including Internet Archive, YouTube, Dailymotion and OK.ru.


## Public sources

Fans and community members find and submit public video links, collection pages and playlist links, along with titles, dates and descriptions. WC Player organises this community-contributed information for browsing and playback. The project accepts metadata and source references, not video uploads, and does not maintain a hosted video archive. Online recordings remain with their external hosts.

Source collection, video and uploader links are shown on media pages where available. Playback and availability depend on the original source.

- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/)
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/)
- [Dailymotion](https://www.dailymotion.com/)
- [OK.ru](https://ok.ru/)

## Reports, corrections and copyright

Report broken links, incorrect metadata or rights concerns using **Report media** where available, or email [wc-complaints@protonmail.com](mailto:wc-complaints@protonmail.com).

Include the title or collection, its original source URL and details of the concern. Rights holders and their representatives should also explain their relationship to the rights owner and identify the material concerned.

We review reports and can correct metadata or remove source references from the player. This does not remove the original recording from its external host.

To request removal of an externally hosted recording, contact its host: [Internet Archive](https://archivesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014759692-Rights), [YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2807622?hl=en), [Dailymotion](https://faq.dailymotion.com/hc/en-us/articles/203657336-Report-a-copyright-infringement) or [OK.ru](https://ok.ru/help/narushenie-avtorskih-prav). You can report concerns about the player's references to us independently.

Adding a link does not grant permission to use the linked material. Rights to recordings, names, logos and likenesses remain with their respective owners.
